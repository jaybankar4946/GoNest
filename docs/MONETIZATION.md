# GoNest Monetization Hypotheses

No pricing or unit economics in this document are validated.

| Model | Payer | Value hypothesis | Main risk | Validation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Agent/broker subscription | Agent/team | Lead and listing workflow efficiency | Low activation or willingness to pay | Offer paid pilot after workflow use |
| Landlord subscription | Owner/landlord | Listing tools, enquiry management, freshness reminders | Infrequent usage | Interview and test a small paid cohort |
| Clearly labeled featured listing | Owner/agent | More qualified visibility | Trust and ranking conflicts | Test with strict disclosure and relevance guardrails |
| Qualified lead fee | Professional | Relevant demand | Disputes about lead quality and regulation | Measure acceptance and downstream outcomes |
| Property-management SaaS | Landlord/manager | Recurring operations workflow | Scope and support cost | Later, after repeated operational use |
| Partner referral | Partner/provider | Relevant customer introductions | Disclosure, conflicts, legal requirements | Counsel review and explicit user disclosure |

## Financial model inputs
Model paying accounts, price, churn, acquisition cost, lead conversion, gross margin, AI cost per task, hosting/storage, support/verification, marketing, payment fees, refunds/losses and staffing. Calculate monthly revenue, contribution margin, burn and runway only from explicit inputs.

Do not conflate revenue, profit, valuation, founder equity value and liquid personal wealth. Do not make an IPO the operating plan.
