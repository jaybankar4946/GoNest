# GoNest Compliance Checklist

This is an issue-spotting checklist, not legal advice or a claim of compliance. Applicability depends on actual product activities, geography, data flows, and commercial arrangements.

## Launch geography decision
Before launch, record target state/city, whether GoNest is only a listing platform or performs brokerage/transaction services, who submits listings, what verification is claimed, and which party contracts with consumers.

## India assessment
Have qualified local counsel assess:
- Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 and relevant state RERA rules, including whether platform activities or particular listings trigger registration/advertising requirements.
- Maharashtra-specific rules if operating in Maharashtra.
- Current commencement and applicability of Digital Personal Data Protection legislation/rules and any other applicable privacy obligations.
- Consumer protection, misleading-advertising and grievance-handling obligations.
- Intermediary/electronic-record obligations where applicable.
- Tax, invoicing, referral, brokerage and payment-provider requirements.

Check current official sources before making claims; do not infer that every provision applies to every marketplace model.

## Product controls to plan
- Clear terms, privacy notice, consent and communication preferences.
- Data minimization, retention and deletion workflows.
- Listing provenance and correction/report mechanism.
- Transparent sponsored placement and paid ranking labels.
- Fair, consistent recommendations; avoid protected-class steering or discriminatory targeting.
- Clear distinction between information and legal/financial/professional advice.
- Accessible complaint, takedown and appeal workflows.

## Release gate
Record counsel/accountant review, owner, evidence, unresolved questions, and re-review date. Do not launch regulated features until the relevant review and provider arrangements are complete.
